Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to Android applications published by LongDragon Technologies on Google Play, unless a specific app provides its own policy.

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Data Collection

Our apps may collect information needed to provide app functionality, improve user experience, diagnose technical issues, and comply with Google Play requirements. The exact data collected depends on each app's features and may include app usage data, crash reports, diagnostics, preferences, and information you choose to provide.

Device Permissions

Some apps may request device permissions such as internet access, notifications, storage, camera, microphone, location, or other Android permissions. Permissions are requested only when needed for app features. You can manage or revoke permissions in your Android device settings at any time.

Advertising

Our apps may display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, app interaction data, and other information to provide, limit, measure, and improve ads. You can manage ad personalization through your Google account and Android device settings.

Third-Party Services

Our apps may use third-party services that collect information according to their own privacy policies. These services may include Google Play Services, Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or similar app infrastructure providers.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information associated with our apps. No digital service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep data access limited to legitimate app operations, troubleshooting, analytics, and compliance needs.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised last updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or an Android app published by LongDragon Technologies, contact us at longdragon.official@gmail.com.
